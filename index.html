Adam El SakkaElectrical Engineer

I’m an electrical engineering student at Université Grenoble Alpes, electronics, energy systems, automatic control and space technology.

I started engineering at Beirut Arab University, in Electrical Power and Machines Engineering. My first year was all foundations: electric circuits, classical physics, electricity and magnetism, calculus, linear algebra and differential equations.

During that year I also worked as a private physics and maths tutor for the Lebanese Baccalaureate, teaching students in Lebanon and abroad, online and in person. Explaining hard ideas simply, week after week, changed how I learn them myself.

In 2026 I was accepted to Sorbonne Université, Université Grenoble Alpes and Université de Bretagne Occidentale. I chose Grenoble for its Electrical Energy and Control Engineering program, where I now study power electronics, automatic control, embedded systems and robotics, in French.

Alongside my studies, I have completed additional training in rocket science through the University of Michigan and MATLAB through MathWorks, and earned the CSWA certification from Dassault Systèmes.

Over the years, I have built a collection of handwritten notes across several courses. I am now digitizing them to make these resources publicly available to students and anyone who may find them useful. I am also writing my first book, Classical Physics, aimed at students, scientists and engineers.
