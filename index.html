About me

I’m drawn to the places where physics, electronics and space technology meet.

I started engineering at Beirut Arab University, where I completed my first year in Electrical Power and Machines Engineering. In 2026 I was accepted to Sorbonne Université, Université Grenoble Alpes and Université de Bretagne Occidentale, and chose Grenoble for its Electrical Energy and Control Engineering program.

Alongside my studies I tutored high-school students in Lebanon and abroad in physics and maths for the Lebanese Baccalaureate. It taught me to break problems down and explain them clearly, which is also why I share my notes and guides here.

Outside class I’ve completed the University of Michigan’s Rocket Science 101, MathWorks’ MATLAB Onramp and Dassault Systèmes’ SOLIDWORKS 3D CAD specialization.
