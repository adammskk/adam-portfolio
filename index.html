        About

        Hi, I’m Adam. I’m an Electrical Energy & Control Engineering student at Université Grenoble Alpes, and I’m drawn to the places where engineering meets physics and space.

        Before Grenoble, I spent a year as a private physics and maths tutor, teaching high-school students in Lebanon and abroad, online and in person, for the GS, LS and SE sections of the Lebanese Baccalaureate. Explaining an idea until it clicks for someone else turned out to be the best way to really understand it myself.

        In 2026 I was accepted to Sorbonne Université, Université Grenoble Alpes and Université de Bretagne Occidentale. I chose Grenoble because its program lines up with the engineer I want to become.

        Outside class I keep stacking skills: the University of Michigan’s Rocket Science 101 specialization, MathWorks’ MATLAB Onramp and Dassault Systèmes’ SOLIDWORKS 3D CAD. I share what I learn on the Guides and Resources pages, so the next student can get there faster.

        I speak Arabic, English and French, and I’m always looking for the next problem where power, control and aerospace meet.

        I hope this page helps. And if you’re working on something in energy, control or space, I’d love to hear about it.
